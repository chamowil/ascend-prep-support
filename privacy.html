Privacy Policy

Ascend Prep app for iPhone · Last updated October 10, 2026

Ascend Prep is an SAT and ACT preparation service operated by Ponte Creation, based in France. This policy covers the Ascend Prep mobile app. The app uses the same account as ascendscores.com, and the website's privacy policy covers the website. We collect only what the service needs to work. We do not sell personal data, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Ponte Creation, the trade name of Wuilmer Jose Ponte Pineda, entrepreneur individuel (SIREN 109 827 014), 75 Avenue Thermale, 03200 Vichy, France. For any question about this policy or your data, email chamowil@outlook.com.

2. What the app collects

  • Account data. Your email address, the name you enter (optional) and your password. Our authentication provider stores the password only as a salted hash, and we never see it.
  • Study data. The exams and drills you take, each answer you give, your scores, and the XP, streak and daily progress the app awards. This is the product: it is how the app scores you and shows your progress.
  • Subscription data. If you buy Premium in the app, Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your payment card details. Your purchase is tagged with your account identifier, and Apple sends us the signed transaction details. From them we learn whether your subscription is active, which product you have and when it renews, expires or is refunded.
  • Technical data. Our servers keep standard request logs (IP address, time and the request made) briefly, for security and to keep the service running.

The app does not use advertising, analytics SDKs or tracking. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

3. Why we use it, and the legal basis

  • To provide the service (performance of a contract): signing you in, running and scoring exams, showing your progress and giving Premium subscribers what they paid for.
  • To manage subscriptions (contract): checking with Apple whether your subscription is active.
  • To keep the service secure and working (legitimate interest): server logs, rate limits and abuse prevention.
  • To answer you when you write to us (legitimate interest).

4. Who processes it for us

  • Supabase (database and authentication), hosted in Ireland, in the European Union.
  • Vercel (servers for the app's API), a US company. The application code runs in Dublin, Ireland.
  • Apple (App Store payments), under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • GitHub (nightly encrypted database backups, kept for 30 days), a US company.

Where a provider is in the United States, transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account and study data: for as long as your account exists.
  • Database backups: a rolling 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Subscription records: as long as needed to provide the subscription and meet accounting obligations.
  • Server logs: a matter of days.

6. Deleting your account and your rights

You can delete your account in the app at any time: Account → Delete account. This immediately and permanently erases your account, results and progress. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to restrict or object to how we use it. Email chamowil@outlook.com from the address on your account, and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to the French regulator, the CNIL, at cnil.fr. If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have equivalent rights. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

7. Students under 18

Ascend Prep is for students preparing for college admissions tests, many of whom are under 18. We do not knowingly allow accounts for children under 13. If you are between 13 and 17, ask a parent or guardian before creating an account or buying Premium. If a parent believes a child under 13 has an account, they can email us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed. Database access is restricted by row-level security so that one student can never read another's data. Backups are encrypted at rest.

9. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top. For a material change, we will tell account holders by email before it takes effect.
