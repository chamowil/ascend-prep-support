Privacy Policy

Ascend Prep app for iPhone · Last updated October 22, 2026

Ascend Prep is an SAT and ACT preparation app published by Ponte Creation, based in France. The app works entirely on your phone: there is no account, no sign-in and no Ascend Prep server. We do not collect your personal data. We do not sell data, show ads or track you across other apps and websites. This policy covers the Ascend Prep app; the website's policy covers ascendscores.com.

1. Who is responsible

The publisher is Ponte Creation, the trade name of Wuilmer Jose Ponte Pineda, entrepreneur individuel (SIREN 109 827 014), 75 Avenue Thermale, 03200 Vichy, France. For any question about this policy, email chamowil@outlook.com.

2. What the app does with your data

  • Kept on your phone only. The name you choose to enter (optional), the questions you answer, your scores and exam results, your XP, streak, badges and quests, your study goal and test date, your settings, and your reminders. All of it is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it or recover it.
  • Notifications. If you turn on reminders, the app asks iOS for permission and schedules them on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server.
  • Subscriptions. If you buy Premium, Apple processes the payment. We never receive your payment card, your Apple Account details or your purchase records. The app asks the App Store on your phone whether a subscription is active. Apple may give us aggregated sales and usage reports through App Store Connect, which do not identify you.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

3. Legal basis

Because the app does not collect personal data, we do not process any through it. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply (legitimate interest) and keep them as long as needed to answer you.

4. Who else is involved

  • Apple (App Store payments and subscriptions), under Apple's own privacy policy.

No other company receives data from the app.

5. How long your data stays

On your phone until you erase it or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its data. Device backups that you make with iCloud or your computer may include the app's data, under your own backup settings.

6. Erasing your data, and your rights

You can erase everything the app has stored at any time: Account → Erase my data. This permanently removes your progress, results and settings from the phone. It does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that in your Apple Account settings.

Since we hold no personal data from the app, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. For anything we do hold (for example, an email you sent us), the GDPR lets you ask to access, correct, delete or export it, and to object to or restrict its use: email chamowil@outlook.com and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to the French regulator, the CNIL, at cnil.fr. If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have equivalent rights. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

7. Students under 18

Ascend Prep is for students preparing for college admissions tests, many of whom are under 18. The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before buying Premium.

8. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage on your phone, protected by iOS's own security and device encryption. Because nothing is sent to a server, there is no Ascend Prep database that could be breached.

9. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top and describe the change in the app's release notes.
