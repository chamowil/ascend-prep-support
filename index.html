Frequently asked questions

What is free, and what does Premium unlock?

The first SAT practice test, the first ACT practice test and the short diagnostics are free, with scoring and an explanation for every question. Premium unlocks every full-length practice exam and every topic drill. It costs US$9.99 per month or US$99.99 per year (the App Store shows the price in your currency).

How do I subscribe, cancel or change my subscription?

Premium in the app is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple Account. To cancel or change it, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or tap Manage subscription in the app's Account tab. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Access continues until the end of the paid period.

I bought Premium but the app still shows it as locked.

Open the Account tab and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you used to subscribe, with an internet connection. If it still does not unlock, email us.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases made in the app are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I already pay for Premium on ascendscores.com. Do I need to pay again?

Yes. The app has no accounts, so the app and the website are separate: Premium in the app is an App Store subscription tied to your Apple Account, and a subscription bought on ascendscores.com does not carry over.

Do I need an account? Will my progress move to a new phone?

You don't need an account or a sign-in. Your progress, results and settings are stored on your phone only, so they do not move to a new phone by themselves and are lost if you delete the app. If you restore a new phone from a backup of your old one, they come along with it. Premium is tied to your Apple Account: on a new phone, open Account and tap Restore purchases.

How do I erase my data?

In the app, open Account → Erase my data. This permanently removes your progress, results and settings from the phone. It does not cancel an App Store subscription, so cancel that in your Apple Account settings.

Are these official SAT or ACT questions?

No. Our questions are original and written to match the format and difficulty of the real tests. Scores in the app are estimates, not official scores.
