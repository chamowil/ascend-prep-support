Support

Help with the Ascend Prep app for iPhone: SAT and ACT practice exams, topic drills and progress tracking.

Contact us

Email chamowil@outlook.com and include the email address on your account. We reply within two business days.

Frequently asked questions

What is free, and what does Premium unlock?

The first SAT practice test, the first ACT practice test and the short diagnostics are free, with scoring and an explanation for every question. Premium unlocks every full-length practice exam and every topic drill.

How do I subscribe, cancel or change my subscription?

Premium in the app is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple Account. To cancel or change it, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or tap Manage subscription in the app's Account tab. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Access continues until the end of the paid period.

I bought Premium but the app still shows it as locked.

Open the Account tab and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Ascend Prep account and the same Apple Account you used to subscribe. If it still does not unlock, email us.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases made in the app are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I already pay for Premium on ascendscores.com. Do I need to pay again?

No. Sign in to the app with the same account and Premium is already active. It stays billed through your ascendscores.com account.

Why was I signed out on another device?

Each account can be signed in on one device at a time. Signing in on your phone signs you out of the website, and the other way round.

I forgot my password.

On the log-in screen, tap Forgot password? and follow the link we email you.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, results and progress. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription, so cancel that in your Apple Account settings first.

Are these official SAT or ACT questions?

No. Our questions are original and written to match the format and difficulty of the real tests. Scores in the app are estimates, not official scores.
